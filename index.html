Before You Begin

Prayer & Scripture

Take a moment to still your heart. God grants wisdom generously to those who ask. These verses and prayers are here to remind you that you do not face this exam alone.

A Prayer Before Your Exam
Lord, Grant Me Wisdom

Heavenly Father, I come before You in humility, trusting that You are the source of all wisdom and understanding. As I prepare to take this exam, calm my anxious mind and steady my hands. Let the knowledge I have studied return to me clearly and in the right moment.

May I not rely solely on my own strength, but lean on You — the God who gives wisdom generously to all who ask. Clear away fear and confusion, and replace them with Your peace that surpasses all understanding.

I surrender the outcome to You, Lord. Whether I do well or face difficulty, let this experience draw me closer to You. Let my efforts honor You. Guide my mind, guard my heart, and be glorified in all I do today.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

A Short Prayer After Your Exam
Thank You, Lord

Father, I thank You for carrying me through this exam. I gave my best, and I trust the rest to You. Whatever the result, I know that You work all things together for good for those who love You.

Help me find peace in Your faithfulness, not in a score. May I continue to grow in diligence and wisdom — not only for exams, but for a life that glorifies You. Thank You for being with me every step of the way.

In Jesus' name, Amen.
